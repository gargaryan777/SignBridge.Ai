1. WHAT IS THIS PROJECT?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SignBridge is a real-time video call platform that watches a
deaf or mute person's hand signs through their camera and
instantly translates them into spoken words — so they can
communicate naturally with anyone, no interpreter needed.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. HOW IT WORKS — THE 3-STEP FLOW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  STEP 1 — INPUT (Camera)
  ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  The user opens their camera in the browser.          │
  │  The app watches their hands in real time.            │
  └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  STEP 2 — PROCESSING (ML Magic)
  ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  A tool called MediaPipe maps 21 tiny dots onto        │
  │  each finger joint to capture the exact hand shape.   │
  │  Those dot positions are sent to our AI model, which  │
  │  was trained on thousands of ISL signs and figures out │
  │  what word or letter the hand shape means.            │
  └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  STEP 3 — OUTPUT (Voice)
  ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  The recognized word appears on screen AND is spoken   │
  │  aloud using a realistic AI voice (ElevenLabs).       │
  │  The other person on the video call hears it live.    │
  └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
3. THE TECH STACK — SIMPLIFIED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  Tool              What it does for us
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  MediaPipe       → Tracks hand joints in camera
  Random Forest   → Our AI that reads hand shapes
  Flask (Python)  → The brain / backend server
  Socket.IO       → Connects two people live
  WebRTC          → Sends the video call peer-to-peer
  ElevenLabs API  → Turns text into natural speech
  Vanilla JS/HTML → Everything you see on screen

4. QUICK PITCH TIPS — HOW TO ANSWER JUDGES CONFIDENTLY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  • If asked "How accurate is it?" →
    Say: "Our model was trained on real ISL gesture data and
    only speaks out a word when it is more than 60% confident,
    so it avoids random wrong guesses."

  • If asked "Why not just use subtitles or an app?" →
    Say: "Most apps are offline, one-way, or need a phone in
    hand. Ours works inside a live video call, hands-free,
    and speaks aloud to the other person in real time."

  • If asked "What's next / how would you scale this?" →
    Say: "We can train it on more signs and full ISL sentences,
    add more languages, and make it a browser plugin so it
    works inside Google Meet or Zoom without any install."
